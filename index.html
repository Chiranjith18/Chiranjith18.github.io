01Machine learningCompetition

Solafune Global Construction Cost Prediction

An international competition to predict construction costs across countries and regions — high-variance targets, messy geography, and a leaderboard that doesn’t forgive leakage.

  • Ranked 12th globally among hundreds of participants worldwide.
  • Built a 2-stage stacked ensemble — a base CatBoost model plus a residual CatBoost — validated with GroupKFold on geolocation groups to prevent spatial data leakage.
  • Engineered target encoding, country–year interactions and a log-relative target transform to tame cost distributions across regions.

02Multi-agent AIReal-time

DebateMind — AI agents that argue

A multi-agent debate platform: two AI agents take opposite sides of a motion, trade arguments in real time, and a judge model weighs the evidence and delivers the verdict.

  • Built with Flask, LangChain and Groq-hosted LLaMA models — two debaters and an impartial judge, streamed live over WebSockets.
  • Document-aware debates: agents ground their arguments in uploaded PDFs, DOCX files, code and images.
